Coverage & methodology

Definitions are part of the analysis

How much volume has economics data?

Volume-weighted reporting coverage · Exact DefiLlama protocol-ID matches per UTC day

Explicit zero counts as reported. Missing protocol-day values remain missing and are never imputed.

Research design

Universe. Use only adapters classified Dexs in the volume API metadata at retrieval time. Exclude aggregators, interfaces, derivatives, prediction markets, and all other categories. This is a current-taxonomy universe, subject to survivorship and taxonomy changes; it is not a historical point-in-time census.

Volume, fees, revenue & matching

Volume is the USD value of trades as reported by each DefiLlama DEX adapter. It is not TVL, unique users, liquidity, or organic volume. Adapter definitions vary and may count routed or arbitraged transactions. Fees are user payments under each reviewed fee adapter's definition; revenue is the portion retained by the protocol and/or token holders rather than supply-side participants. Revenue is not profit. No fee or revenue value is inferred from volume.

Join breakdown names to that endpoint's unique metadata and then match defillamaId across endpoints. No fuzzy name joins or parent-level fee allocation. HumidiFi (ID 6554) is excluded from both economics metrics because its adapter estimates inventory P&L. Negative revenue from rebates or distributions is preserved as reported. Economics totals sum available values for matched DEX IDs; fee and revenue cohorts can differ. Basis points use only pairs with both volume and the relevant economics value: 10,000 × Σ metric / Σ matched volume. Coverage is Σ volume with metric reported / Σ observed volume. No annualization or token-price attribution.

Shares, HHI & growth decomposition

Protocol shares use each parent's observed volume divided by the same day's total observed DEX volume. Aggregate adapter versions by DefiLlama parentProtocol, falling back to protocol ID. Never add parent totals to child totals. HHI uses all parent groups, including groups drawn in Other: 10,000 × Σ s²; CR5 is the sum of the five largest shares. The leaderboard retains adapter granularity.

Growth rates compare sums of consecutive, non-overlapping 7d or 30d windows. Decomposition includes only adapters with 60 explicit daily volume observations. On that stable cohort, ΔVᵢ = sᵢ₀ × ΔM + M₁ × (sᵢ₁ − sᵢ₀). Effects reconcile exactly to each group's USD change and to total cohort change. This is an accounting identity, not causal attribution; entry, exit, and incomplete reporters are outside the cohort. Cohort coverage is displayed above.

Anomalies, missingness & validation gates

For each day, compare log(1+volume) with the median of the previous 30 calendar days, excluding the scored day. Require at least 20 prior observations. Robust scale is max(1.4826 × MAD, 0.10). Flag only if absolute robust z is at least 4 and absolute deviation from the back-transformed median is at least 50%. The log-scale floor avoids division by zero. A flag calls for investigation; revisions, outages, or new coverage can produce a flag.

Fail the build on missing expected API calendar days, duplicate dates or ambiguous IDs, nested schema changes, negative volume, unreviewed negative fees, nonfinite observations, stale end dates, no matched DEX data, unmapped labels above 0.5% of absolute endpoint USD, or breakdown-versus-global-total gaps above 0.5% (USD 1 absolute tolerance). Reconciliation checks the entire endpoint before category filtering and matches the API headline convention: sum positive breakdown cells, excluding negative adjustments. Our DEX revenue sums preserve signed adjustments and can therefore differ from a headline-style sum. Missing protocol-days are retained as missing; explicit zeros are retained as zero. Observed totals, shares, and HHI remain sensitive to unreported activity. Current and future UTC days are excluded.
