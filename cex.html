Interpret the limits before comparing

Daily volume measures executed activity; depth measures displayed resting interest at one moment. Taker buy ratio describes which side demanded immediacy, not new capital inflows, unique buyers or a trading signal. A book imbalance can change before execution. Read the financial definitions and sampling contract before drawing conclusions.

V3 · Intraday activity and volatility

Verified historical minutes: 2026-08-15 through 2026-11-12; 524,160 rows including one warmup day across four pairs.

Research Question

Which UTC hours exhibit concentrated activity and intraday risk?

Hypothesis

Activity and squared-return intensity vary by UTC hour and weekday.

Method

Equal-minute average quote volume and RMS minute log return grouped by UTC hour and ISO weekday. Daily realised volatility is sqrt(sum of minute squared close-to-close returns). Daily signed taker flow is 2 × taker-buy quote / total quote − 1. No seasonality significance tests.

Robustness

Every expected minute, UTC boundary and checksum is validated. Observation counts make unequal weekday frequencies explicit. Daily association tests below control weekday indicators.

Limitations
